AnnoyBlock

Version 1.0.0

AnnoyBlock replaces hard website blocking with configurable, friction-based UX degradation. Instead of denying access, it makes distracting content less engaging through subtle effects like click delays, image rotation, and text blurring — all grounded in behavioral psychology research.

Key Features

  • 10-level configurable annoyance scale (0-9)
  • Per-domain customization with whitelisting
  • 6 distinct effects: saturation, click delay, font weight, image rotation, text blur, cursor jitter
  • Focus Mode scheduler for automatic work-hour activation
  • SPA and infinite scroll compatible
  • Manifest V3 compliant with minimal permissions
  • Under 2MB memory footprint per tab

Behavioral Psychology

AnnoyBlock is grounded in Fogg's Behavior Model, Duhigg's Habit Loop, and Thaler & Sunstein's Nudge Theory. By adding small amounts of friction rather than blocking access entirely, it weakens habitual browsing patterns without triggering psychological reactance.
